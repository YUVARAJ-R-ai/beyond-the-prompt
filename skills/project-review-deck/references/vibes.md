# Vibe Presets

Each vibe maps to a concrete palette, font pairing, and motif. Pick the one the user chose and apply it on **every** slide — committing to one system is what separates a designed deck from AI-generated filler. Colors are hex (no `#`). All font choices are from the pptx skill's QA-safe list unless noted.

Apply the pptx skill's universal rules regardless of vibe: dark title + closing slides, lighter content slides; one dominant color (60–70%), supporting tones, one accent; no accent stripes/underlines; every slide has a visual element.

---

## Professional / Corporate  *(default for graded or panel reviews)*
Clean, trustworthy, conservative. Safe choice for an academic or sponsor review.
- **Palette:** primary `1E2761` (navy) · secondary `CADCFC` (ice blue) · accent `028090` (teal) · text `212121` on white
- **Fonts:** headers **Cambria** (serif), body **Calibri**
- **Motif:** thin icons in solid navy circles next to section headers
- **Backgrounds:** title/closing navy; content white

## Modern Startup / Pitch
Bold, confident, high-contrast — for demo day or a competition.
- **Palette:** primary `0F0F0F` (near-black) · secondary `F2F2F2` · accent `F96167` (coral) OR `02C39A` (mint) — pick one and commit
- **Fonts:** headers **Arial** bold (heavy, large 40–44pt), body **Calibri**
- **Motif:** oversized stat numbers (60–72pt) in the accent color; full-bleed accent block on title
- **Backgrounds:** title near-black with accent; content white with accent highlights

## Technical / Engineering
Dark, diagram-forward, for an audience that wants to see the system.
- **Palette:** primary `0D1117` (dark slate) · secondary `1C7293` (teal-blue) · accent `02C39A` (mint) · text `E6EDF3`
- **Fonts:** headers **Calibri** bold, code/labels **Courier New**, body **Calibri**
- **Motif:** monospace labels for components; diagrams get the most slide real estate
- **Backgrounds:** dark throughout (premium engineering feel); ensure ≥ 4.5:1 text contrast

## Aesthetic / Editorial
Refined, spacious, design-led. Fewer elements, more whitespace.
- **Palette:** primary `36454F` (charcoal) · secondary `E7E8D1` (sand) · accent `B85042` (terracotta) · text `2B2B2B`
- **Fonts:** headers **Century Schoolbook** or **Bookman Old Style** (serif), body **Calibri**
- **Motif:** generous margins, one focal image or diagram per slide, italic accent taglines
- **Backgrounds:** off-white content; charcoal title/closing

## Academic / Research
Structured, restrained, figure-and-caption oriented — for a research review.
- **Palette:** primary `21295C` (midnight) · secondary `F2F2F2` · accent `065A82` (deep blue) · text `1A1A1A`
- **Fonts:** headers **Cambria**, body **Calibri**; captions 10–11pt muted
- **Motif:** numbered figures with captions; clean tables; minimal decoration
- **Backgrounds:** white content; midnight title

---

If the user names a vibe not listed here (e.g. "playful", "dark neon", a brand color), build a palette in the same shape — one dominant, 1–2 supporting, one accent — and a matching safe-font pairing, then apply it just as consistently.
